    Worksheet Product Rule Worksheet
    

    Objectives

    • State the product rule for differentiating a product of two functions.
      🔗
      🔗
    • Apply the product rule to compute derivatives of products involving polynomials, trigonometric, and exponential functions.
      🔗
      🔗
    • Recognize when the product rule is needed, and when it is not.
      🔗
      🔗
    🔗
    When a function is built by multiplying two simpler functions, we cannot just multiply their derivatives. The product rule tells us what to do instead.
    🔗

    Definition 1.

    If \(f\) and \(g\) are differentiable functions, then
    \begin{equation*} \frac{d}{dx}\bigl[f(x)\,g(x)\bigr] = f'(x)\,g(x) + f(x)\,g'(x). \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    Let’s work through an example.
    🔗

    Example 2. Applying the product rule.

    Find the derivative of \(h(x) = x^2 \sin(x)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Let \(f = x^2\) and \(g = \sin(x)\text{,}\) so \(f' = 2x\) and \(g' = \cos(x)\text{.}\) By Definition 1,
    \begin{equation*} h'(x) = f'(x)\,g(x) + f(x)\,g'(x) = 2x\sin(x) + x^2 \cos(x). \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    1. Practice.

    Use the product rule (Definition 1) to find the derivative of each function.
    🔗

    \(p(x) = x^3 e^x\)
