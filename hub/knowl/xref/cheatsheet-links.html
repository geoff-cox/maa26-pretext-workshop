External link

<url href="https://abc.com">
  click here
</url>
Internal cross-reference
🔗
<xref ref="def-x"/>
<xref ref="def-x" text="title"/>
<xref ref="def-x" text="custom">see here</xref>
The target must have an xml:id. Default link text is auto-generated (e.g., “Definition 3.1”); text="title" uses the target’s title; text="custom" lets you write your own link text between the opening and closing tags.
